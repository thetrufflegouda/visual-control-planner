# Changelog

## [1.0](https://github.com/thetrufflegouda/visual-control-planner/releases/tag/v1.0) — 2026-11-26

- Visual binding plans with a hardware library, input capture, live diagrams, and custom controller layouts.
- Shareable plans omit local paths and controller pairings. The app asks before using explicit network or Windows device paths from opened plans.
- The Device Library opens on All Devices with All manufacturers selected and loads previews as cards come into view.
- The binding diagram opens with every hardware view expanded, respects manual collapses during live edits, and shows saved routed actions and their timing.
- Native profile editing for supported games, including per-device DCS profiles and SWG Space keymaps.
- Profile exports recheck destination files before writing and roll back failed paired writes.
- Gremlin routing with vJoy button and axis auto-mapping, a patch bay, and hold or toggle shift modifiers.
- Combined Gremlin and game-profile export for Star Citizen, War Thunder, and SWG Space.
- Windows x64 portable package with a single executable, a `Support` folder, and the MIT license for VCP's code.
