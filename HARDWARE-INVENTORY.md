# Flight and space sim hardware inventory

Updated November 24, 2026. This inventory covers the major consumer and enthusiast makers of Windows flight and space sim controls. It includes current hardware and widely used legacy models, from HOTAS and swappable grips to yokes, pedals, throttle quadrants and cockpit panels. Regional bundles and accessories are recorded where they change layout or input routing.

A manufacturer gallery identifies the actual hardware and usable camera angles. A manual or control diagram establishes the physical inputs. Windows identifiers and control numbers require a factory mapping or a live capture. Rows with gaps identify the evidence still needed. VCP's Device Library shows completed layouts.

## Layout and verification notes

- Map physical controls by model, hand and installed module. A VKB SCG Premium with the optional C1 push button and one with the C1 hat need different control layouts. Some profiles also change the A1 input and the rapid-fire trigger.
- Pair each layout to the endpoint the hardware exposes. X52 stick and throttle occupy one USB controller. X56 stick and throttle each occupy a separate USB controller. A passive stick base contributes the ordinary X/Y axes under its grip. Bases with their own switches, throttles or encoders contribute a separate control view.
- Use the full stock control diagram and more than one angle when hardware occludes inputs. Mode switches may change firmware button signals. Specialized cockpit panels may need a separate Windows HID handler or a supported simulator integration.
- Prioritize the original Warthog stick and throttle, F/A-18 and F-14 grips, X52/X52 Pro and X56, T.Flight Rudder Pedals (TFRP), MFD Cougar and Logitech flight panels, the WINCTRL Orion/URSA configurations, and the documented VIRPIL and VKB hardware variants.

- Keep side configurations such as SCG C1 side push versus hat and A1 digital versus analog as selectable physical inputs. Label-only and included-detent changes can share stable control IDs. Handed VIRPIL/VKB image assets need a left/right photograph or documented mirror check.
